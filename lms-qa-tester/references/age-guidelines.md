# Age-suitability guidelines

Judge the content against the target group below. Rate each scorecard criterion from 1 to 5,
where 5 is an ideal fit, and quote the slide text behind every concern.

Estimate the reading level from a sample of at least 3 slides per module. Use average
sentence length, the share of words with 3+ syllables, and unexplained jargon. Report it as
an approximate age or school grade, not as an exact score.

| Group | Target reading level | Sentence length | Vocabulary | Concepts & examples | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Children 8–12 | Age 8–11 (UK Y4–Y7 / US G3–G6) | ≤ 12–15 words | Everyday words, and new terms always defined | Concrete, one idea per slide, school/home/play examples | Fear or violence, unsafe online behaviour, asking for personal data, ads or purchases, sarcasm, abstract theory |
| Teens 13–17 | Age 12–15 (Y8–Y11 / G7–G10) | ≤ 15–20 words | Subject terms OK if explained | Some abstraction, with examples from school, social media, games, part-time work | Gambling, alcohol/drugs, explicit or romantic content, body image, risky challenges, data privacy, a talking-down tone |
| Young adults 18–24 | Age 15–18 | ≤ 20–25 words | Professional terms OK, explain niche jargon | Applied, careers/university/first-job examples | Assumed work experience, outdated references, patronising tone |
| Adults / professionals 25+ | Age 16+ | ≤ 25 words | Industry terms fine | Workplace scenarios, ROI, decision making | Over-simplification, filler, unsupported claims, outdated tools or stats |

Every group:
- **Inclusivity**: stereotypes, culturally narrow examples, gendered defaults, and imagery that excludes people.
- **Accuracy**: outdated facts, dates or tools (note the year that was claimed), and claims with no source.
- **Cognitive load**: more than ~100 words on a slide for children or teens, or more than ~150 for adults; walls of text; several new ideas at once.
- **Visuals**: whether the images are age-appropriate, relevant, and readable on a small screen.
- **Tone**: consistent and respectful, and not too childish or too academic for the group.

Verdict rules:
- **Not suitable**: there is any sensitive-content problem for under-18s, or the reading level is more than 3 years above the target across most modules.
- **Suitable with changes**: there are issues that local rewrites can fix.
- **Suitable**: only minor issues.
